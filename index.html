How to install.

01

Back up

Save your photos, contacts and chats. Flashing wipes the phone.

02

Unlock bootloader

Unlock your device's bootloader using the official method for your phone.

03

Flash recovery

Install the recommended custom recovery for your device.

04

Wipe and flash

Format data, then flash the ROM zip from recovery.

05

Reboot

The first boot can take a few minutes. Then set up and enjoy.

Flashing a custom ROM can void your warranty and erase all data. You do it at your own risk, so always make a full backup first.

/faq

Questions.

Is this the official HyperOS?

No. It is an unofficial community ROM and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xiaomi.

Will I lose my data?

Yes, a clean install wipes your phone. Take a full backup before you start.

Something is not working.

Reflash the ROM after a clean wipe first. If the bug stays, report it with your device name and a short description.

Can I go back to stock?

Yes. Flash your device's stock firmware to return to the original software.
